# SOP: IT Incident Management
Process: IT Incident Management
Roles: Service Desk Agent, ITSM Platform, Incident Manager, Network Team, End User
Domain: IT Service Management
Version: 2.1

## Purpose
This procedure defines how IT incidents are logged, prioritized, resolved and closed. It covers all incidents reported by end users.

## Procedure

1. The process begins when an incident is reported by an end user.
2. The Service Desk Agent logs the incident in the ITSM Platform.
3. The ITSM Platform automatically categorizes the incident and assigns the priority.
4. The Service Desk Agent investigates the issue and documents the diagnosis in the ticket.
5. If the incident is major, the Incident Manager opens a war room.
6. Otherwise, the Service Desk Agent applies the standard fix.
7. In both cases, the Incident Manager informs the business stakeholders while the Network Team restores the affected service.
8. If the issue persists, the Service Desk Agent escalates the ticket to the Incident Manager and the process returns to issue investigation.
9. Otherwise, the Service Desk Agent verifies the resolution with the End User.
10. The End User confirms the fix.
11. The Service Desk Agent closes the ticket in the ITSM Platform.
12. The ITSM Platform sends the satisfaction survey to the End User.
13. The ITSM Platform archives the ticket and the process ends.

## Controls
Major incidents require a post-incident review within five working days.
