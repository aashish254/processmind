# SOP: Employee Leave Approval
Process: Employee Leave Approval
Roles: Team Lead, HR Officer, HR System, Payroll Team
Domain: Human Resources
Version: 1.1

## Purpose
This procedure defines how employee leave requests are checked, approved and recorded.

## Procedure

1. The process begins when an employee submits a leave request in the HR portal.
2. The HR System checks the leave balance automatically.
3. If the balance is insufficient, the HR System rejects the request and the process ends.
4. Otherwise, the Team Lead reviews the request.
5. If the leave exceeds ten days, the HR Officer requests a backfill plan.
6. Otherwise, the Team Lead approves the leave request.
7. In both cases, the HR System updates the leave calendar and sends the confirmation to the employee.
8. The Payroll Team records the deduction in the payroll run and the process ends.

## Controls
Leave balances are synchronized with the payroll system every night.
