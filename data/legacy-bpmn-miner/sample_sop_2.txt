Purchase Request Procedure

1. Submit the purchase request through the procurement portal.
2. Validate the budget line with the finance controller. If the amount is
   above the monthly threshold, obtain additional sign-off from the CFO.
3. Select a vendor from the approved vendor list.
4. If no approved vendor matches the request, run a request for quotation
   with at least three vendors and select a vendor afterwards.
5. Create the purchase order and send it to the vendor.
6. Meanwhile, register the expected delivery date in the ERP system.
7. Confirm the delivery when the goods arrive at the warehouse.
8. Register the invoice when it arrives from the vendor.
9. Check the invoice against the purchase order. If the invoice does not
   match the purchase order, resolve the discrepancy with the vendor and
   register the invoice again.
10. Pay the invoice on the next payment run.
