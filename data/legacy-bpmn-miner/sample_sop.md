# Employee Onboarding Procedure

This document describes the standard onboarding process for new employees.

## Step by step

1. Receive the signed offer from the candidate.
2. Create the employee record in the HR system.
3. If the employee is a contractor, request the external badge from security.
4. Otherwise, request the internal badge from security.
5. In parallel:
   - Provision the laptop with the IT team.
   - Create the email account with the IT team.
6. Notify the hiring manager once the equipment is ready.
7. Schedule the orientation session with the new hire.
8. If the new hire starts remotely, send the welcome package by courier.
9. Otherwise, prepare the desk assignment with facilities.
10. Verify the completed checklist and archive the onboarding file.

## Notes

The HR coordinator owns the process end to end. If any step fails, the HR
coordinator escalates to the HR manager and the onboarding restarts from the
employee record step.
