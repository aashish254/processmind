# SOP: Vendor Onboarding
Process: Vendor Onboarding
Roles: Procurement Officer, Compliance Team, Finance Manager, Vendor Portal, ERP System
Domain: Procurement
Version: 1.4

## Purpose
This procedure defines how new suppliers are registered, screened and activated in the procurement system. It applies to all direct and indirect spend categories.

## Procedure

1. The process begins when a vendor submits a registration application through the Vendor Portal.
2. The Vendor Portal automatically verifies the completeness of the submitted documents.
3. If the application is incomplete, the Vendor Portal notifies the vendor to resubmit the documents and the process returns to document verification.
4. Otherwise, the Procurement Officer reviews the application in detail.
5. The Compliance Team performs background screening while the Finance Manager prepares the financial risk assessment.
6. If the screening fails, the Procurement Officer rejects the application and the process ends.
7. Otherwise, the Finance Manager evaluates the financial documents.
8. The Finance Manager approves the vendor request.
9. The Compliance Team approves the compliance findings.
10. The ERP System creates the vendor record and sends the welcome pack to the vendor.
11. The Procurement Officer enters the vendor category in the ERP System.
12. The ERP System archives the application file and the process ends.

## Controls
Approvals must be recorded in the ERP System with a timestamp. Screening evidence is retained for seven years.
