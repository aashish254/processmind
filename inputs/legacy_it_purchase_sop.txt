Legacy Procedure — IT Equipment Purchase Approval
(revised 2019, owner: purchasing, scan of signed original on file)

The procedure described herein governs the acquisition of IT equipment by
business units. It has been applied since 2017 and supersedes memo P-112.
Requests for exceptions are to be directed to the procurement department in
writing.

Basically how it works these days: an employee fills in the purchase
requisition form and sends it to the IT procurement mailbox.

The requisition is logged into the tracking sheet by the procurement
department, and they also attach the vendor quote to the case folder.

Once logged, it gets checked by the Finance Controller for budget coverage.
Should the budget not be sufficient, the requester is notified by the
procurement department and the whole thing is sent back to them to revise the
amount, after which it is checked again by the Finance Controller.

Note: orders above 5000 EUR additionally require a sign-off by the CFO. This
note was added after the 2021 audit finding.

The IT Manager then reviews the technical specification. In practice they
sometimes skip this when it is a repeat purchase, but officially the review is
mandatory.

After both reviews are done, the purchase order is raised in the ERP by
procurement and emailed to the vendor by the same person.

Goods receipt is confirmed by the requester upon delivery, the invoice is
matched by Accounts Payable, and payment is released by them after matching.
End of procedure; the case folder is archived by the procurement department
once payment has been released.
